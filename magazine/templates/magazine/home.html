Welcome to The ISFDB Explorer

Bibliographic data on speculative fiction: science fiction, fantasy, and horror. More than 2 million titles, 200 thousand authors/artists, and 82 thousand magazine issues, using a local copy of the Internet Speculative Fiction Database.

The Internet Speculative Fiction Database (ISFDB) is a massive collection of bibliographic data on speculative fiction publications in print, electronic, and audio formats. The first version of the web site went live in 1995, and it is continually updated by a large community of volunteer database editors. The project and its history are described on Wikipedia.

This web site — The ISFDB Explorer — gives you access to a local copy of the ISFDB database, providing an alternative means for viewing the data using a different user interface. The most recent and most complete data is always available at ISFDB. The data used here is from a backup copy of the ISFDB database downloaded in February 2026, so any additions and changes made to ISFDB since then are not included in our output.
